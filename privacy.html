Privacy notice

Last updated: September 2026

This notice explains how Profimedia ("we", "us") handles personal data in connection with this website and when you contact us. We keep this simple: we collect as little as possible.

This website

  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • Fonts and all other assets are served from this website. No third-party content is loaded.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. As with any website, these providers may process technical data such as your IP address and browser details in server logs, to deliver and protect the service. See the GitHub privacy statement and the Cloudflare privacy policy.

When you email us

If you contact us at info@profimedia.co.uk, we will receive your name, email address and anything you choose to include in your message. We use this only to respond to you and, if we work together, to manage that relationship. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries or, where relevant, taking steps to enter into a contract.

We do not sell or share your data for marketing. We keep correspondence only for as long as needed for these purposes, or as required by law (for example, accounting records).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. To exercise these rights, email info@profimedia.co.uk.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
