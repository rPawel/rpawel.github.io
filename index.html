About us

Small by design

Profimedia is an independent consultancy of a few experienced developers and DevOps engineers, based in London. We are not a large agency and we don't pretend to be one.

That works in your favour. There are no account managers or sales layers: you speak directly to the engineers who design, build and support your systems. Decisions are quick, communication is straightforward and the people who build it are the people who answer the phone.

Our background covers both software development and operations, so we understand what development teams need from a platform, and what it takes to keep it running at 3am.

  • Certified Kubernetes AdministratorCKA certification from the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) and The Linux Foundation.
  • Senior, hands-on engineersMany years of running production systems, from single servers to multi-cluster platforms.
  • You own everythingAll code, configuration and documentation live in your repositories. No lock-in, ever.
  • London-based, remote-firstWorking with clients across the UK and Europe.

FAQ

Common questions

How big is Profimedia?

We are deliberately small: a few experienced developers and DevOps engineers. You work directly with the engineers doing the work, with no account managers or sales layers in between.

Do you only work with cloud platforms?

No. We work across public cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), on-premises and bare-metal environments, and hybrid set-ups that combine both.

Can you help with an existing Kubernetes cluster?

Yes. We regularly review, upgrade, secure and stabilise existing clusters, as well as build new ones. Our team includes a Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).

Do you offer ongoing support and patching?

Yes. Alongside one-off projects we offer ongoing arrangements covering OS and application patching, Kubernetes upgrades, monitoring and general infrastructure maintenance.

Do you work remotely?

We are remote-first and based in London. We work with clients across the UK and Europe, and can meet on site in London when useful.
